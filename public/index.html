⚖️ Your Statutory Rights under RBI:

Under the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (CICRA), Indian credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian) and banks must resolve credit report disputes within 30 days. Failing this, the borrower can escalate directly to the RBI Banking Ombudsman.
